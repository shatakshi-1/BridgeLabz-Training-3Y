My favorite hobby is reading books. I especially enjoy thrillers, mysteries, suspense , and stories that keep me guessing until the very end. Some of my favorite books are The Silent Patient, Never Lie, and Verity, as I really enjoy their suspenseful plots, unexpected twists, and interesting characters. Silent Patient, in particular, is a psychological thriller built around a mystery and a shocking act of violence.

Apart from thrillers, I have also read books like Ikigai and The Alchemist, which have a more inspirational and philosophical feel. Reading is not just a hobby for me; it helps me relax, improves my imagination, and allows me to explore different ideas, emotions, and perspectives through every story.
